First Goshen Gate School

"Nurturing Tomorrow's Leaders Today"

A leading nursery and primary school committed to providing quality education in a safe and stimulating environment where every child is encouraged to discover their unique potential and develop a lifelong love for learning.

What We Offer

Quality Curriculum

A well-structured curriculum aligned with national standards, blending academic excellence with creative and moral education to develop the total child.

Experienced Staff

Our dedicated and passionate teachers bring years of experience in early childhood and primary education, creating engaging learning experiences.

Safe Environment

A secure, nurturing and child-friendly space where every student feels valued, protected, and encouraged to express themselves.

Modern Facilities

Well-equipped classrooms with modern teaching aids, computer labs, a library, and outdoor play areas for holistic development.

Inclusive Community

We celebrate diversity and welcome children from all backgrounds, fostering a culture of respect, empathy, and teamwork.

Co-curricular

Beyond academics, we offer sports, arts, music, clubs, and events that build confidence and uncover hidden talents.

Our Mission

To provide a strong educational foundation that equips every child with the knowledge, skills, character, and confidence to thrive in an ever-changing world.

Our Vision

To be the school of first choice in the community, recognized for academic excellence, moral uprightness, and all-round student development.
